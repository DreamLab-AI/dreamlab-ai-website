How It Works

Every engagement follows a structured process from discovery through deployment, with clear milestones and deliverables at each stage.

1

Discovery

We map your challenge, assess technology readiness, and identify the right combination of expertise. No commitment required.

2

Scope

Define deliverables, team composition, timeline, and success criteria. We identify funding opportunities including Innovate UK grants and KTP schemes.

3

Build

Your team embeds in the lab alongside our specialists. Hands-on co-creation with daily standups and working prototypes from day one.

4

Validate

Test against real data, real users, and real constraints. We apply the AREA Responsible Innovation framework to anticipate downstream impacts.

5

Deploy

Handover of validated prototype, documentation, and deployment roadmap. Post-engagement support ensures continuity.
